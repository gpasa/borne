PC games

Borne recognizes these games by their data files, wherever they are in your PC games folder. File names can be upper or lower case. Borne doesn't provide any game: these files come from your copy (Steam, GOG, original CD or DVD).

Doom 3 and Quake 4

GameFiles needed
Doom 3base folder with pak000.pk4 to pak004.pk4
Resurrection of Evild3xp folder with pak000.pk4, even if installed elsewhere
Quake 4 (Apple silicon Macs)q4base folder with pak001.pk4 to pak012.pk4 and a voice file such as zpak_english.pk4

Engines: dhewm3, included; openQ4, to download. Original DVDs need id Software's updates (Doom 3 1.3.1, Quake 4 1.4.2), which Borne downloads once you accept their license; the Steam and GOG versions already include them.
