The arcade

Borne drives MAME 0.289, included in the app. It knows every machine in MAME; the ones whose ROMs you have are in the Disponibles (available) folder.

ROM folders

Settings → Arcade → Dossiers ROM. Point to the roms folder itself, or to the folder that contains it: Borne finds roms inside. Your sets are .zip files (one per game, named with MAME's short name, such as pacman.zip); the hard disks and CDs of more recent machines are folders of .chd files with the same name.

Add ROMs, then rescan the folders: Jeux menu → Relire les dossiers de jeux (⌘R). Borne also rescans on its own when a folder's contents change.

A clone (a regional version, a revision) often needs its parent, and some machines need a BIOS: keep them in the same folder. The game's details show its parent.

Finding a game

  • Search: type part of the title, the short name or the manufacturer in the field at the top right.
  • Sort: click a column header (game, year, manufacturer, status…).
  • Filter: the folders on the left — originals or clones, working games, horizontal or vertical screen, games that can save their state — and Regrouper par (group by) manufacturer, year, driver or CPU.
  • Favorites: the star in the details (⌘D) adds the game to the Favoris folder.

The État (status) column shows MAME's verdict: Bon the game runs well, Imparfait it is playable with flaws (sound, colors…), Préliminaire it does not work yet.

Keys

Borne launches MAME with a layout designed for a Mac keyboard. Each game's details list its exact keys; here are the essentials:

5Insert a coin
1Start, one player
← ↑ → ↓Joystick
Z X C V B NPlayer 1 buttons 1 to 6
6 then 2Player 2 coin and start; joystick on R D F G, buttons on A S Q W
MouseSteering wheel, trackball or light gun
PPause
F12Screenshot (it becomes the game's picture in the details)
EscQuit the game

Why not Ctrl to fire, as on a PC? On a Mac, Ctrl + arrow opens Mission Control or switches desktops, right in the middle of a game. So the buttons are on Z X C…

Playing without the external drive

Installer sur ce Mac… (install on this Mac), in the details, copies the game with everything it needs: its parent, BIOS, devices and samples. The size is shown before copying. Jeux menu → Installer les favoris sur ce Mac… does the same for all your favorites.

Local copies always come first: the game starts even with the drive unplugged. They are in the Sur ce Mac folder; Supprimer la copie removes one.

Diagnosing a game

A game won't start? In its details, Diagnostiquer (diagnose) asks MAME for everything it needs and checks it: the game itself, its parent, its BIOS, its devices, its sounds, then every file. Each line says what is there (✓) and what is missing (✗), with what to do: which set to add, which to replace with its MAME 0.289 version. Files nobody has ever found a good copy of are flagged as such: their absence isn't your doing.

Checking your ROMs

Jeux menu → Vérifier les ROM (⌘⇧R) asks MAME to check every set you have. The check runs in the background, verdicts appear as they come, and ⌘. stops it whenever you like.

Game pictures

The details show a picture of the game: screenshot, title screen, marquee, flyer, cabinet or control panel, your choice. Point Settings → Arcade → Images to the folder that holds the snap, titles, marquees, flyers, cabinets or cpanel subfolders. Without them, F12 during the game makes one.

Display and per-game settings

  • Rendering: Settings → Général → Rendu de MAME, Metal (bgfx, the default) or OpenGL.
  • Extra options: the Options en plus field passes options to MAME, for example -bgfx_screen_chains crt-geom for a CRT look.
  • A shifted or distorted picture? MAME's menu (Tab) may have changed the game's display. In the details, Réinitialiser l'affichage clears that setting and nothing else; Réinitialiser tous les réglages… resets the game completely (its saves are kept).
