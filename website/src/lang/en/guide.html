Getting started

Ten minutes to install Borne, show it your games and launch the first one. The tabs above then cover the arcade and PC games in detail. Borne's interface is in French for now; this guide names each item as it appears in the app.

1. Install Borne

  1. Download the version for your Mac: Apple silicon (M1 chip and later) or Intel. Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac.
  2. Open the downloaded .zip file, then drag Borne to your Applications folder.
  3. Launch Borne. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without a warning. macOS 14 or later is required.

Nothing else to install: MAME 0.289 and the PC game engines are inside the app. A few modules, whose licenses don't allow bundling them, download from the settings (see PC games).

2. Show Borne where your games are

Open the settings (Borne menu → Réglages, or ⌘,).

  • Arcade → Dossiers ROM (ROM folders): choose the roms folder, or the one that contains it. Your ROM sets are .zip files there.
  • Jeux PC (PC games): Ajouter un dossier… (Add a folder) and choose the folder that holds your PC games. Borne looks through its subfolders and recognizes each game by its files.

These folders can be on an external drive. Borne only reads them: it never writes anything there, neither settings nor saves.

3. The Borne window

On the left, the folders
Tous les jeux (all games), Disponibles (available: the ones whose files you have), Favoris, Sur ce Mac (on this Mac); then one folder per PC game (Doom, Quake…); then arcade machine features (originals, clones, working games, vertical screen…) and Regrouper par (group by) manufacturer, year, driver or CPU.
In the middle, the list
Click a header to sort. The search field, top right, filters by title, short name or manufacturer as you type.
On the right, the details
A picture of the game, the Lancer (play), Favori and Installer sur ce Mac… buttons, the game's features, and its keys.

4. Play

Double-click a game, or select it and press ⌘O. At launch, a reminder shows the keys for a few seconds; they also stay in the game's details. Esc quits the game and takes you back to Borne.

Games open full screen, or in a window if you tick Dans une fenêtre (Settings → Général).

5. Where your data goes

Everything the games write (per-game settings, saves, high scores, screenshots, recordings) goes to your user folder, ~/Library/Application Support/Borne by default. Settings → Données lets you move it and show it in the Finder.

The games drive is never modified: you can unplug it, lend it or share it between several Macs.

6. Updating

Since version 1.7, Borne updates itself: every day it checks GitHub for a new version, offers it, then replaces the app and relaunches it. Every update is signed: a version that doesn't come from Borne is refused. Borne menu → Rechercher les mises à jour… (check for updates) checks right away; Settings → Général can also install them automatically, without asking.

Before 1.7, just replace the app in Applications with the new version: your settings and saves are kept.
